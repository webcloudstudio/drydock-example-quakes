FEATURE: Top

FieldValue
Version20261024 V1
DescriptionThe top report: the largest events by magnitude, limited by --n.
Depends OnFEATURE-MinMagFilter.md, FEATURE-ExitCodes.md
Providesquakes top
Consumesfilter_min_mag(events, m), format_time

Purpose

quakes top [--min-mag <m>] [--n <count>] <file> writes the header time,mag,depth_km,place,id and the --n largest events ordered by mag descending, then time ascending. --n is a positive integer defaulting to 10; 0, a negative number or a non-integer is a usage error (exit 2). Magnitude and depth have one decimal (depth rounded half up). Rows are written with the csv module, so a place containing a comma is quoted, and as UTF-8.
